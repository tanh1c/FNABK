# Campus doors and workshop pass

AC-01, AC-02, AC-03 and AC-09: AI-assisted change, pending student review. `CampusDoorSetup` configured 70 B1 classroom leaves, 336 existing outer room doors and 19 workshop shutters. `B1MainDoorSetup` added two leaves at the single main entrance. `CampusInteriorDoorPass` matched 471 corridor openings to furnished rooms and added doors and numbered plates; nearest-geometry matches need human verification. `CampusRoomNumberSync` matched 326 outer labels to room IDs and marked 10 ambiguous entries `ACCESS`. `WorkshopDetailPass` added native equipment detail to 7 C1, 6 C2 and 6 C3 stations. The recorded floor-count attributes for all 23 buildings were unchanged.

`CampusDoors.server.luau` runs on the server: E toggles the door, range and collidable line of sight are checked, and a door will not close on a humanoid. Moving leaves temporarily have collision disabled. The service configures 898 doors; target-device performance has not been profiled. Number plates describe generated room IDs, not verified real-world room numbers. Other buildings retain their existing interiors; structural inventory is not proof that every interior is complete or architecturally accurate. In particular, Canteen currently contains classroom-style room models and needs a dedicated canteen redesign.

Executed: Rojo build succeeded; edit-mode `CampusDoorCheck` passed. In controlled single-player Studio Play with test-only repositioning and, where noted, an anchored character and controlled camera, a B1 classroom door, B1 main leaf, B2 outer door, B2 inner door, and C2 shutter were each observed changing state; B1 main leaf, B2 inner door, and C2 shutter were closed and reopened. The C2 shutter refused to close while the test avatar's hands occupied its volume, then closed when the avatar moved away. These samples do not establish correctness of all 898 doors or multiplayer behavior.

Manual Studio test: approach doors from both sides, press E to close and reopen, and try closing while a player or NPC stands in the doorway; expected: the server changes state and collision, and refuses an obstructed close. Repeat at B1, a classroom on each floor of A/B/C buildings, and each C1-C3 workshop shutter. Walk stairs, lobbies and mission routes with solo and two clients; expected: prompts remain distinguishable, signs identify the correct room, and no route soft-locks. Inspect all generated room labels and workshop layouts; profile frame rate and server performance on the target device separately.

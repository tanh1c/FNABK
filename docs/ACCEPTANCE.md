# Survival design — acceptance and test plan

These criteria trace the current [five-day survival GDD](GDD.md). **All are NOT TESTED for the new design.** Detailed values labeled as proposals in the GDD remain provisional. The running source is the older A01 prototype, not proof of survival-system implementation. Historical A01 AC-01–AC-11 and their original evidence are preserved in [A01_ACCEPTANCE](archive/A01_ACCEPTANCE.md); those identifiers and results do not transfer to these criteria.

| ID | Requirement | Manual Studio test and expected result | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| SURV-01 | A solo player can play the five-day survival-to-rescue loop | Start fresh runs, choose a base, acquire supplies, complete power/frequency/transmitter objectives in different orders without secrets, signal and board. Expect locations chosen randomly from buildings suitable to each task, their exact positions shown on the map, variation across runs, stability on save/resume, and a reachable solo rescue and individual result. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-02 | Five-day structure with 0–2–2–3–4 warned night waves | Advance controlled runs with 1–4 players, including cases where players do not kill attackers quickly and where a player joins or leaves mid-wave. Expect five 12-minute days, no base wave on night 1, 2 on nights 2 and 3, 3 on night 4, 4 on night 5, night durations of 5–5–5–6–8 minutes, readable warnings and increasing danger. In solo, expect 10, 14, 18 and 24 total attackers per wave on nights 2–5; each extra eligible player increases the solo count by 50%, rounded up. Each wave uses the number of connected living or downed players at its start, excludes eliminated spectators and retains that total and cap until the next wave. Active wave enemies, including those retreating until they leave, must not exceed 20, 30, 40 or 50 for the wave's 1–4 player snapshot, or the total wave count. Earlier attackers retreat on schedule to allow remaining spawns while nights end on time. Test solo defensibility and target-device performance. Exact timing, waiting-to-respawn and zero-eligible-player edge cases, and stuck-enemy recovery remain open. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-03 | Any of the 23 campus buildings can be selected as a base | At every building, claim the base, inspect power/storage/defense options and traverse entry, exit and respawn route. Expect all 23 supported and no inaccessible routes. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-04 | Relocate with physical supplies; retain building upgrades | Upgrade building A, move selected items to B, claim B, then return to A. Expect transported items at B, unmoved items and fixed upgrades at A, with no duplication or loss. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-05 | Gradual food/water needs, inventory, health, stamina and simple crafting | Confirm both needs start at 100, food decreases by 1/minute and water by 1.5/minute, and one or both at zero causes 1 HP total loss per 5 seconds; verify food +35, water +40, medkit +40 HP, all capped at 100, and 8 slots with at most 5 same-type items per slot; then craft and repair. In solo and co-op, collect food, water and ammunition across multiple days and verify day 1 uses initially placed supplies and limited replenishment starts at day 2, then occurs at the start of each new in-game day at suitable locations that vary by day, with no map markers for these points, and increased supply for larger groups. Leave supplies of different types uncollected across a replenishment and verify they remain while their combined stock at each point stays within the shared cap of 10 items, counting each food portion, water bottle or ammunition box as one. Attempt a pickup with a full inventory and no compatible stack space: expect the item to remain in place and an inventory-full message. Verify any player can pick up and share replenished supplies; have two players request the same item simultaneously and expect exactly one recipient and an already-taken message for the other. Provide insufficient capacity and verify priority is water, then food, then ammunition; when not all ammunition fits, verify types alternate, the starting type rotates on successive replenishment days pistol → shotgun → rifle and repeats, and filled type allocations are skipped, starting with pistol on day 2. Save and resume after replenishment and verify the same day is not replenished again and the rotation stays unchanged; fill every selected point and verify unplaced replenishment is discarded rather than carried to another day. Change the connected party before the new day and verify replenishment uses the connected player count at day start; join afterward and verify no additional allocation until the next daily replenishment. With adequate capacity, expect food totals 3 / 5 / 6 / 8, water totals 4 / 6 / 8 / 10, and ammunition box totals 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 connected players respectively. For solo verify 2 pistol, 1 shotgun and 1 rifle box. For co-op, verify pistol/shotgun/rifle box allocations of 3/2/1, 4/2/2 and 5/3/2 for 2, 3 and 4 connected players respectively when capacity allows. Verify the active base may be selected but receives no more than floor(25% of the total items actually replenished that day), with supplies also placed in other areas; test insufficient capacity and rounding. Exact locations remain open. Expect correct server-owned values and feedback; selected and proposed numbers need playtesting. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-06 | Mimics can impersonate NPCs and player characters with fair clues | In solo and co-op, encounter both forms and inspect unusual appearance or behavior, then verify with UV or a scanner. Expect an opportunity to investigate or avoid them before attack without requiring a tool to notice a tell or assuming access to real player voice/chat. The proposed number and exact design of clues need testing. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-07 | Distinct experimental-machine and ambushing-creature behaviors | Approach a machine using light/sound detection and a hiding creature while quiet and noisy; fire each gun with enemies just inside and outside its 60 / 100 / 120-stud hearing radius, including with a wall between shooter and enemy, then inspect multiple base waves. Expect enemies inside to be attracted regardless of walls, those outside not attracted by that shot, no scheduled daytime base wave, legible cues and solo-accessible counterplay, with mutated creatures as most wave attackers and machines/Mimics appearing occasionally. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-08 | Validated combat, friendly fire and recovery | Start with a melee weapon, find a pistol, shotgun, rifle and their distinct ammunition types; verify loaded capacities of 12, 6 and 30 rounds and full boxes of 20, 10 and 30 rounds respectively, with at most five same-type boxes per inventory slot. Reject nonmatching ammunition. From a full box, load an empty pistol, shotgun or rifle once; expect 8, 4 or 0 rounds respectively left in the box. Verify minimum shot intervals of 0.4, 1.2 and 0.15 s and reload durations of 2 s, 0.7 s per shell and 2.8 s respectively; holding fire automatically shoots the rifle, each pistol or shotgun press fires only once, and the server rejects firing faster. Interrupt shotgun reloading after one or more shells and fire; expect the loaded shell to be consumed once, remaining box rounds preserved, and no duplication. Move or stack the box and save/reload; expect unused rounds and loaded ammunition to persist without duplication or loss. Against an enemy, confirm 25 HP pistol and 35 HP rifle base damage; confirm six shotgun pellets of 10 HP each at or below 15 studs, linear falloff to 5 HP each at 40 studs and 0 at or beyond 65 studs, and proportional partial-hit damage. With 2 and 4 clients, hit a teammate with the baton and with each firearm; confirm 5 HP per hit/shot including a hit that downs at 0, and confirm multiple shotgun pellets apply at most one 5-HP hit per teammate per shot. Test limited-ammo firearm use, reload and forged shots, along with range/cooldown/forged requests. Revive within 30 seconds using a 5-second hold and confirm 40 HP and unchanged food and water. Revive with either need at zero and verify the selected 1 HP per 5 seconds total damage continues. Begin a hold too late to finish before the deadline: expect expiry to trigger respawn or spectator state as applicable. During another hold, attack the rescuer or move them out of interaction range and verify the hold interrupts and restarts from zero; separately allow the window to expire. Respawn with a prior need above 50 and another below 50; expect immediate automatic respawn at the active base with 100 HP, exactly 50 food, exactly 50 water, consumption of one of three uses and an item bag at the downed location. Test damage immunity for 5 seconds, then repeat and attack earlier: expect immunity to end on attack; both the owner and teammates can take items. With a living teammate, down a player who has zero respawns: revive them within 30 seconds and expect 40 HP; repeat without a revive and expect spectator mode on expiry. Down the whole group while at least one player has a respawn remaining: expect the run to continue until their automatic respawn. Exhaust all group respawns and down everyone: expect group defeat and a new run required to play again. Repeat solo, test concurrent retrieval and item ownership; expect server validation and no duplication or unexpected loss. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-09 | Solo through four-player cooperation | Complete representative objectives with 1, 2 and 4 players, including split-up play and one disconnect. Expect consistent shared objectives and no mandatory multi-person interaction. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-10 | Host-owned world save and resume | Save a changed base, supplies, objectives, time and player state; close and reopen as host with guests returning. Have the host leave and verify a save is attempted, the session closes, and the host can reopen the successful save. Unexpectedly disconnect the host: expect a 60-real-second return window with game time and dangerous activity paused and movement, inventory changes and other gameplay actions locked. Verify remaining players see a waiting-for-host message and countdown. During helicopter boarding, disconnect the host and verify the departure countdown and any still-valid vote countdown pause; if the host was aboard, expect removal from the boarding roster and cancellation of an active vote. Save while the helicopter is waiting and reopen the run: expect the helicopter and its remaining boarding time restored, the remaining boarding countdown starting when the host enters, players recorded aboard at the successful save restored aboard if they rejoin before departure, and any prior vote canceled with a new vote available. Leave a previously aboard player disconnected through departure and expect Disconnected, not Rescued. Verify the saved roster reflects removals made before saving. Reconnect the host within the window and expect immediate continuation from the paused state; repeat without a return and expect a save attempt and closure, including during boarding. Repeat with a simulated failed save: expect the group to be informed and only the last successful save to be available. Pause implementation and delivery to a disconnected host remain open. Disconnect a living non-host while the session runs: expect their character to disappear safely, their inventory to remain inaccessible to teammates merely because of disconnection, and the player to reappear at the active base on reconnect. Repeat while downed: expect their character to remain visible and revivable, with the 30-second timer and normal revive, respawn or elimination rules continuing, including a teammate-accessible bag on respawn. Reconnect before the timer expires and expect the player to remain downed at that location with the remaining time. In another run, revive the disconnected player: expect a group notification, their character to disappear safely and reappear at the active base when they reconnect. Separately exhaust their respawns and let them be eliminated while disconnected: on reconnect to the continuing run expect spectator mode. Verify food and water do not decrease and zero-need damage pauses while disconnected, then resumes on reconnect if either remains zero. Compare a teammate revive with an automatic respawn while disconnected: only the latter grants 5-second immunity starting on reconnect, ending earlier on attack. Reconnect and verify the server-recorded state updated by these events and any subsequent valid transfers, without recreated items. After the session closes, expect recovery from the last successful save, with no promise to retain unsaved changes. Interrupt transfers and simulate failed writes. Leave the run closed and reopen it: expect the game clock and replenishment not to have advanced while closed and resume from the last valid save. Expect one owner/location per item and visible save failures. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-11 | Individual helicopter rescue outcomes | Restore power, find the frequency and transmitter components and signal before night 5; survive night 5 and wait at the sports field. Repeat with the prerequisites completed and signal sent within 10 minutes after night 5; also let the 10-minute deadline expire without signaling and expect a loss. If signaling is delayed, verify up to three base attack waves start at minutes 1, 4 and 7 after night 5, with 24 / 36 / 48 / 60 total attackers per wave for 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 eligible players respectively, subject to the selected active caps; for solo expect 20 creatures, 3 machines and 1 Mimic per complete wave, and both an audible warning and a text notification 30 seconds before each wave. For co-op, verify creature/machine/Mimic totals of 30/5/1, 40/6/2 and 50/8/2 for 2, 3 and 4 eligible players respectively. Leave attackers alive at grace-wave transitions and expect retreat to begin at 3:30 and 6:30 after night 5, with attackers gone before the next wave spawns; if the final wave starts, expect its retreat at 9:30. Test stuck attackers both outside all player views and still visible at the scheduled start: expect the former removed out of view and the latter to disappear with an effect before the next wave starts on time. Signal after a grace-wave warning but before its start: expect the wave to be canceled and a cancellation notification. Signal during an active grace-period wave and expect it to continue on its original retreat schedule while no further grace-period waves start; repeat after retreat begins and expect retreat to continue. When signaling before the end of night 5, expect no new base waves during the helicopter wait, with already active enemies still dangerous. Spawn pacing and retreat completion remain under review. Board only some players and test majority departure and timeout. Verify all connected players see the three-minute boarding countdown. Before a vote begins, verify the initiating boarded player sees all group members not aboard, including downed, eliminated and disconnected statuses, and can decide whether to proceed. Change the boarding roster while the warning is open and verify the list updates before confirmation. Have the initiator leave the helicopter and expect the warning to close and boarding to be required before initiating again. Leave the warning open at the three-minute deadline and expect immediate departure. Verify any boarded player can start a departure vote, automatically casts one yes vote, and can change or withdraw their vote before departure or the vote ends; verify it lasts at most 10 seconds. Reach a strict majority before expiry and expect immediate departure; with only one player aboard, expect immediate departure when they initiate the vote. Let another vote expire without a strict majority and expect continued waiting; start a vote just before the three-minute boarding timeout and expect immediate departure at the timeout; have someone board or leave during a vote and expect cancellation and the ability to start a new vote. Disconnect a boarded non-host before departure: expect removal from the boarded and voting rosters and cancellation of an active vote; reconnect before departure and expect them at the active base. Attempt to carry a downed player aboard and expect boarding to be unavailable until revived; leave one downed outside at departure and expect Left Behind. Test enemy, friendly-fire and zero-need damage while aboard the landed helicopter: expect immunity. Leave the helicopter before departure and verify the player can return to help teammates and no longer has helicopter protection. Set up a player already downed aboard and verify they are rescued if still aboard at departure. Let the three-minute boarding window expire with nobody aboard and expect group defeat. At departure include a disconnected player and a connected eliminated spectator: expect Disconnected and Eliminated results respectively, neither rescued. Expect arrival about 4 minutes after both the signal and night 5, departure after at most 3 minutes or early majority approval by players aboard, and individual outcomes matching who boarded. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-12 | Accessible presentation, deliverable and measured performance | Inspect HUD, menus, cues and settings; walk all required routes and secrets; profile realistic 1–4-player scenes on target hardware. Expect readable cues and documented results; ≥30 FPS is a target until measured. | NOT TESTED |
| SURV-13 | Survivor NPCs and Trust | Meet survivor NPCs and obtain information, supplies and an optional side quest; verify they do not follow the party. Confirm group Trust starts at 100, stays within 0–100 and decreases by 10 on every client for a validated hit or shot against innocent NPCs; a single shot hitting multiple innocent NPCs or multiple pellets must cause only one −10 penalty. Help a survivor or complete a side quest and confirm each valid help/completion restores 10, capped at 100. Check repeatable help types appropriate to the NPC: delivering supplies, repairing an item or clearing an obstacle. On each in-game day, verify at least one NPC in the school offers a repeatable help task requiring no supplies and still respects the per-NPC daily Trust limit. Replay a side quest and reload the same run: expect that quest to grant Trust only once in the run, including after resume. Verify normal communication and trade at 70–100; at 30–69, offers contain 50% of the normal quantity rounded down, at least one item if stock exists and zero if empty; at 0–29, no trade with essential communication only. Rescue information remains available at all tiers; Trust recovery through repeatable help is subject to the once-per-NPC-per-day limit. NPCs may defend themselves when directly attacked and stop after the attacker stops attacking and stays more than 20 studs away continuously for 10 seconds; test interruption of that condition. Confirm a survivor NPC has 100 maximum HP; reduce an innocent NPC to 0 HP and expect temporary incapacitation and automatic recovery after 60 seconds at 50 HP, not permanent death. Low Trust alone must not trigger an unprovoked attack. Expect server-owned state. Other non-quest help qualification and multi-attacker or disconnect handling remain open. | NOT TESTED |

## Prototype milestone and evidence

For SURV-13, also repeat a non-quest help action for the same NPC using different players on one in-game day, including after save/resume. Expect at most one +10 Trust award for the group from that NPC that day, with eligibility reset on the next in-game day.

First implement or review one testable subsystem at a time: graybox area with one Mimic and a traceable Chase/Search transition, then server-validated baton combat. A one-building vertical slice validates the loop but does **not** replace the selected 23-building base scope, additional enemies or four-player goal.

For each change record the relevant SURV IDs, exact Studio steps, configuration, date, observed results, logs or video, and remaining limitations. Do not mark tests passed from code inspection, prior A01 user reports, an unpublished preview, or an unexecuted plan. Proposed damage, wave schedules, rescue timings and rates require team review and playtesting before being treated as locked.
